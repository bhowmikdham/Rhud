**Panel verdict (compounding-moat strategic bets)**

The three real moats are all *substrate-present but loop-open*: the per-tenant ML pipeline exists end-to-end but its dispatch and outcome-feedback edges are deliberately cut; the cryptographic audit chain is fully built but never sealed and never exported; institutional scoping memory has the capture substrate (provenanced ledger + INSERT-only thread) but no reuse layer. That is the strongest possible position for a strategic bet: the hard part is built, the moat is dark.

They click `rhud.link/g/a1b2c3…` — no account, no password, just a clean card: "Have a scoping sheet? Drop it in." They have an old Excel asset list, so they drag it in. A spinner: "Parsing your scoping document… usually 5-15 seconds." The page polls every 2 seconds. A Review modal pops: "From your document — we read 3 items," each tagged Strong / Approximate / Borderline. They skim, fix one wrong endpoint count, then walk the decision tree: single-selects, a long-text, file attachments. They hit a loop — "Web App 1," questions 1 of 5 — finish it, get "Add another web app?", click Add, repeat for the API. The sidebar tracks 18 answered. They hit Submit; a warning: "Submitting locks the link. You won't be able to edit after." They confirm. "Scope received — your rep will come back within 24 hours." Then silence. No tracking, no portal. They wait, refreshing email, until a proposal eventually arrives.

**Friction moments:**
- [4] Rep's "Copy link" silently failed (verified audit bug) -> buyer gets a dead/no link, sees "This link doesn't work" -> "Just give me one that opens."
- [3] Submit irreversibly locks the link -> they hesitate, re-read every row, or email the rep to reopen -> "Let me fix a typo after sending."
- [3] After "Scope received," zero visibility for ~24h -> they refresh inbox, sometimes re-ping the rep -> "Show me where my quote is."
- [2] Borderline auto-fills risk rubber-stamping a wrong number -> they squint at chips, second-guess -> "Tell me exactly what you couldn't read."
- [2] No save-confirmation if they close mid-tree -> uncertainty it persisted -> "Reassure me it's saved."

**Biggest time sink:** The opaque post-submit wait for a proposal with no status or ETA beyond "24 hours."

**Felt experience:** Brief delight at the doc-prefill, then anxiety over the locking Submit, then dead-air waiting.

**Dream outcome:** One link that always opens, edit-until-the-rep-reviews, a live "your quote is being priced" status page, and a proposal in minutes — not a day-long black box.
